## Overview

KL Rahul produced the most aggressive full season of his IPL career in 2026, combining his familiar volume scoring with a scoring rate far above his own baseline. Across 14 innings for Delhi Capitals, he scored 593 runs at an average of 45.62 and a strike rate of 174.41, finishing fourth on the Orange Cap table at the end of the league stage.

That scoring pace is the story of his season. Rahul has been one of the IPL's most consistent accumulators for close to a decade, but the long-standing criticism of his tempo largely disappeared in 2026, with his strike rate climbing more than 35 points above his career figure of roughly 139. Delhi finished sixth and missed the playoffs, which made his return the standout individual campaign in an otherwise flat season for the franchise.

## Key highlights

Rahul's IPL 2026 campaign was defined by the reinvention of his tempo. The run tally was in line with his usual output, but the strike rate and boundary count showed a batter scoring with far more urgency than his career record would suggest.

Some of the main takeaways from his season:

- **Career-best aggression** — a strike rate of 174.41 was comfortably the most attacking full-season figure of his IPL career.
- **Record-setting hundred** — his 152 not out is the highest individual score by an Indian batter in IPL history.
- **Volume intact** — 593 runs placed him fourth in the Orange Cap race at the close of the league stage.
- **Carried the batting** — he was Delhi's leading run-scorer for the second season running, in a side that finished sixth.
- **Finished on a high** — a 30-ball 60 against Kolkata Knight Riders at Eden Gardens closed out the season after Delhi had already been eliminated.

## Shreyas Iyer — IPL 2026

Shreyas Iyer captained Punjab Kings for a second season in 2026 and again batted with the freedom that has characterised his time at the franchise. He scored 498 runs in 13 matches at a strike rate close to 170, including one century and five fifties, while playing his 100th IPL match as captain during the campaign. Punjab finished fifth and missed the playoffs a year after reaching the final.
